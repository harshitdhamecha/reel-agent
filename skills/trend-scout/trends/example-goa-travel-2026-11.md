## Patterns
- **7–12 s** total for the top performers.
- **Flash-montage opener**: 15–18 highlights at 0.10–0.17 s in the first ~2–2.5 s, then 0.4–1.0 s beat cuts.
- **One small, static, thin-serif caption** for the whole reel, upper area, often + emoji row. Invitation phrasing is common ("Goa together?").
- Bright daytime travel content dominates: plane window, palms, beach, scooter POV, shacks, food, Portuguese houses.
- Songs: international dance/afro-house hooks, Latin trending audio, or original audio.
